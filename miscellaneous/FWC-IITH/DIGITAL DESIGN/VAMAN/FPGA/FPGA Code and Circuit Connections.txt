ARDUINO CIRCUIT CONNECTIONS:
1. **LCD Display Pins:**
   - RS to Arduino pin 4
   - RW to Arduino pin 5
   - E to Arduino pin 6
   - D4 to Arduino pin 7
   - D5 to Arduino pin 8
   - D6 to Arduino pin 9
   - D7 to Arduino pin 10
   - VSS to GND (Ground)
   - VCC to 5V (Power)
   - VEE (Contrast) is connected directly to GND (No potentiometer)
   - LED+ to 5V through a current-limiting resistor (e.g., 220 ohms)
   - LED- to GND (Ground)

2. **Arduino Uno to Breadboard:**
   - Connect Arduino GND (Ground) to the breadboard's ground rail.
   - Connect Arduino 5V to the breadboard's positive rail.

3. **Backlight Resistor:**
   - Connect one leg of the resistor to LED+ pin of the LCD.
   - Connect the other leg of the resistor to 5V rail on the breadboard.

4. **LCD Contrast Adjustment (VEE):**
   - Since you're not using a potentiometer, directly connect VEE pin of the LCD to GND.

Ensure the connections are secure and correct. With this setup, your Arduino Uno should be able to control the LCD display and show the values of R1, R2, and R3 as per the provided code.
